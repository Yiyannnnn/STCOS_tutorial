Generative structure

For spot \(j\), gene \(g\), and cell type \(c\), ST-COS first defines a spatial mean \(\mu_{jg}^{(c)}\) and a negative-binomial size parameter \(\theta_g^{(c)}\). Selected genes are coupled through a Gaussian copula:

\[ W_j^{(c)} \sim N\left(0, \rho^{(c)}\right), \qquad Y_{jg}^{(c)} = F^{-1}_{\mathrm{NB},g,c} \left\{\Phi\left(W_{jg}^{(c)}\right)\right\}. \]

Here, \(\rho^{(c)}\) is the user-specified latent correlation matrix and \(Y_{jg}^{(c)}\) is a cell-type-specific average expression level at that spot. It is not an observed count from one individual cell.

For spot-based data, the final profile is the abundance-weighted mixture

\[ X_{jg} = \sum_c n_{jc}Y_{jg}^{(c)}, \qquad n_{jc}=N_jp_{jc}, \]

where \(p_{jc}\) is the cell-type proportion and \(N_j\) is the total cell-equivalent abundance for the spot.

This construction models variation among cell-type-specific spot profiles. It does not simulate within-spot cell-to-cell variability or sum independently sampled individual cells.

Two simulation modes

Reference-free

The user supplies or generates:

  • coordinates;
  • cell-type proportions;
  • hotspot, streak, layer, or non-spatial mean patterns;
  • negative-binomial means and sizes; and
  • one latent dependence matrix for each cell type.

This mode is appropriate when a benchmark requires known truth or a deliberate change to a spatial or dependence parameter.

Image-guided reference-free design

The ST-COS Shiny app extends the reference-free workflow with histology-image upload and segmentation, externally supplied coordinates, and interactive spatial-region controls. It can be opened from R with launch_stcos_app(). A local image-coordinate designer is available through shiny::runApp(stcos_shiny_app()).

Reference-based

get_real_param() estimates:

  • gene-wise negative-binomial parameters;
  • spatial mean trends for genes passing the Moran’s I criterion;
  • a thresholded latent dependence matrix; and
  • diagnostics for positive-definite matrix repair.

The current reference-based workflow estimates one global spot-level dependence matrix. It does not infer cell-type-specific co-regulation from a heterogeneous spot mixture. Cell-type-specific matrices must be supplied from an external source or specified in reference-free mode.

Output orientation

generate_true_count() returns a named list containing one spot-by-gene matrix per cell type. generate_spot_count() returns one spot-by-gene abundance-weighted spot matrix. Reference counts supplied to get_real_param() must instead have genes in rows and spots in columns.
